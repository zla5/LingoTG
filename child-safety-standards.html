Public policy page for Google Play Child Safety Standards compliance.

1. Our commitment

LingoTG (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We do not permit CSAE/CSAM on our services. We take reports seriously and cooperate with applicable laws and competent authorities.

LingoTG provides messaging and translation-related features. Users may communicate using accounts compatible with third-party messaging infrastructure. This page describes the child-safety standards that apply to LingoTG’s product experience, membership services, and our response process.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Any sexual content involving minors (anyone under 18), including real, AI-generated, fictional, or cartoon depictions
  • Grooming, solicitation, or sexual exploitation of minors
  • Sharing, requesting, storing, or linking to CSAM
  • Any attempt to use LingoTG to facilitate CSAE

Violations may result in removal of content where we control it, suspension or termination of LingoTG membership access, device restrictions, and referral to relevant authorities where required by law.

3. Age requirements

LingoTG is not directed to children. Users must meet the minimum age required by applicable law in their country/region and the age requirements of any underlying messaging account they use. We do not knowingly allow children to create LingoTG membership accounts for paid translation services.

4. In-app reporting and blocking

Users can take action against unsafe content or accounts by:

  • Using in-app Report tools on chats, users, channels, or media where available
  • Using Block to stop further contact from a user
  • Contacting LingoTG child-safety support (details below) for membership-related or product-related concerns

When reporting CSAE/CSAM, please include: a description of the issue, relevant usernames or links, approximate time, and any screenshots that do not further distribute illegal material beyond what is necessary for reporting.

5. How we handle reports

  • We review child-safety reports with priority
  • We preserve relevant records as needed for investigation and legal compliance
  • We may restrict LingoTG membership features or terminate access for violators
  • Where required, we report apparent CSAM to appropriate regional or national authorities / hotlines (for example, NCMEC or local equivalents)
  • We may share necessary information with law enforcement in response to valid legal process

6. Legal compliance

We aim to comply with applicable child-safety laws and regulations in the jurisdictions where we operate, including obligations to report CSAM to relevant authorities. Users must also comply with local laws. Nothing in this page limits rights or obligations under mandatory law.

7. Education and prevention

  • We publish these standards publicly so users and reviewers can understand our rules
  • We train staff who handle trust & safety / membership support to escalate CSAE reports
  • We continuously improve reporting paths and response procedures

We may update these standards from time to time. The latest version will remain publicly available at this URL. Continued use of LingoTG after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised standards where permitted by law.
